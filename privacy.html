Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This is a general starter policy — please have it reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it for a live business.

1. Information we collect

To process a booking we collect passenger names, contact details, passport/ID numbers, and payment information. We also collect basic usage data to keep the site working well.

2. How we use it

Your information is used to search and confirm flights, send booking confirmations and e-tickets, respond to support requests, and comply with airline and legal requirements.

3. Who we share it with

We share the details needed to issue your ticket with the operating airline and our payment processor. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including secure payment processing.
