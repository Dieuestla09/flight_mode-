About us

Built to make East African travel simpler

Fly-Mode Flight was started in Kigali with one goal: give travelers a single place to search, compare, and book tickets across the region's airlines, instead of checking each one separately.

We work with RwandAir, Kenya Airways, Ethiopian Airlines, Uganda Airlines and other partner carriers to bring real fares and schedules into one simple search, so booking a trip takes minutes rather than hours.

Fly-Mode Flight is run by NDAHAYO DIEU EST LA and based in Kigali, Uganda, with support available by phone, WhatsApp, and Instagram for travelers across the region.
